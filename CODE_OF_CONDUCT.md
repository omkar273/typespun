# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of
participation.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should communicate constructively, assume good intent while
addressing impact, accept feedback gracefully, respect differing viewpoints,
and keep technical disagreements focused on the work. Helping newcomers and
giving clear context are strongly encouraged.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing private
communications without permission are not tolerated.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and whenever someone is representing
the project in public or private community interactions.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report conduct concerns privately, contact the repository owner using a
private contact method they publish on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/omkar273). Do not open a public issue that
identifies affected people or includes private communications.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality as far as
practical, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may range from a
private correction or warning to temporary or permanent exclusion from project
spaces. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
